
📊 Superstore Analytics Dashboard | Tableau Public

📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Superstore Analytics Dashboard using Tableau Public to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, and regional sales across the USA.

🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Understand product category and sub-category performance.
* Compare sales across geographical regions.
* Explore customer segment contributions.
* Identify top-performing products and monthly sales trends.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Tableau Public
* Microsoft Excel
* Data Visualization
* Business Analytics

📈 Dashboard Features

* KPI Cards: Sales, Profit Margin, and Return Rate.
* Sales by Category: Compare performance across product categories.
* Sales by Sub-Category: Identify high-performing sub-categories.
* Sales by State: Explore geographical sales distribution.
* Sales by Segment: Compare Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
* Top 5 Products: Highlight leading products by sales.
* Sales Trend: Track monthly sales performance.
* Interactive Filters: Explore data by metric, region, and order year.
